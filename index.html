About

I am a researcher in formal methods, with a focus on mechanised proofs and interactive theorem proving. My work bridges the gap between mathematical reasoning and machine-checked verification, aiming to make formal verification more accessible and scalable.

My research interests include interactive theorem provers (Isabelle, Coq, Lean), program verification, certified compilers, and the formalisation of mathematics.
